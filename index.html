Porbandar Auction Cricket

An online cricket player-auction project created by Vikram Odedara.

🏏 Online Player Auction

Porbandar Auction Cricket is designed to help cricket tournament organizers conduct live player auctions with team owners participating in player bidding.


  1. Organizer creates a tournament.
  2. Teams are configured.
  3. Players are added.
  4. Virtual auction points are assigned.
  5. Player base prices are set.
  6. Live bidding begins.
  7. Teams use virtual points to purchase players.

💡 Current Project

The project uses virtual points for cricket-auction bidding. The auction points are intended for the tournament auction system and are not real-money betting.
